The plan

Four pillars.
In the order they make money.

01 · Convert on visit one

Sell the plan, not the visit

Packages, memberships and consults become bookable online. A scripted "here's your plan" conversation before every first-timer leaves. Day-one package rate 15% → 22%.

+$26K a month
02 · Retain

The second visit

Automatic return offer inside 14 days. Membership pitch at visit two. Renewal reminder before a package runs out. Runs on Podium; email and SMS live here, segmented, with a job on every send.

+$34K rebooking · +$20K members
03 · Acquire

More of the good ones

Paid search and local search on the treatments that make the money, where Google allows it. Referral programme. Source captured on every first booking. Paid social stays a test.

+$24K a month
04 · Measure

Count only what was used

Code or link on every campaign. Revenue attributed only when a guest redeemed it. Two numbers on your phone every week: day-one package rate, second-visit rate.

September · the proof

Ten days of $10 Botox
tests the whole machine.

Botox is 2.4% of your revenue, which is exactly why it is the safe place to prove the funnel: new patients only, coded link, second-visit offer and the "here's your plan" conversation in the room. If it works on Botox, we point it at hyperbaric, EmSculpt and IV, where the money is. SpaDerma, two streets away, already runs this offer from its own page.

01
New patients only

At $10 a unit it sits at cost. Sent to your list it discounts revenue you already had.

02
Its own page, coded link

Every redemption counted automatically, per channel. No influenced revenue.

03
Visit two before they leave

A dollar off per unit if they rebook, and the plan conversation. This is the number we're testing.

04
Measured daily

Redemptions, new patients, day-one attach, rebooked. A number every morning.
